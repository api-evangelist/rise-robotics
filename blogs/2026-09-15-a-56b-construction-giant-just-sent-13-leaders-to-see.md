Last week, a senior delegation from Groupe Bouygues, a ~€56B French industrial group with major construction and infrastructure businesses, spent the morning touring our lab in Somerville. Thirteen leaders made the trip, organized by MIT's Office of Corporate Relations, including their Group Chief Innovation Officer and technical heads from across their construction and infrastructure divisions. The head of their construction group was particularly excited by the potential for our technology to replace hydraulics in heavy machinery.
