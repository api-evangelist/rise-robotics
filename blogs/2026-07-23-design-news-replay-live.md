Last week, I joined Physik Instrumente and igus, both expert motion control manufacturers, for a live webinar hosted by Design News on the technology shaping the next generation of machine design. We discussed where motion control is headed as machines take on more precise work in more demanding settings, from factory floors and warehouses to operating rooms and off-road equipment. Engineers are pushing for energy-efficient systems that boost performance, while interest is growing in AI and digital twin integration.
