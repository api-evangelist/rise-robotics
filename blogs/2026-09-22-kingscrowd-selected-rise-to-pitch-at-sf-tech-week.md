Kingscrowd selected RISE as one of only a handful of companies to pitch live at their Startup Showcase in San Francisco, right in the middle of SF Tech Week – and you’re invited to Wefunder’s HQ to watch! Kingscrowd has built a standout data-driven research platform, objectively tracking and rating thousands of crowdfunding deals and companies. They rated RISE a top deal with a 4.6 out of 5, and they invested.
