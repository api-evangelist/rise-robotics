Some news worth celebrating: RISE Robotics has been awarded a START grant from MassVentures, funded by the Commonwealth of Massachusetts! 🎉 The Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) Targeted Technologies (START) program exists to help deep tech companies turn federally funded research into commercial products. Out of a record number of applicants this year, 26 Massachusetts startups were selected.
