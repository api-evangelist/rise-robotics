Many of you backed RISE because you believe in step change technologies that disrupt legacy mechanical systems. I recently met Andrei Evulet, co-founder and CEO of Jetoptera, and his team is applying that same instinct to aviation. Jetoptera has built the Fluidic Propulsive System® (FPS®), a bladeless propulsion architecture that replaces the loud, dangerous propellers of helicopters and other vertical takeoff and landing aircraft.
