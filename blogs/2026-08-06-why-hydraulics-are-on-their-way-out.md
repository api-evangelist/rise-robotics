Did you know that RISE Robotics started as an exosuit concept before it became an industrial actuator? When RISE’s co-founders set out to build a real-life Ironman suit while studying at MIT, the plan didn’t include inventing a powerful technology that could make hydraulics obsolete. Hydraulics have dominated heavy machinery since before the automobile, so this is an extraordinary leapfrog innovation!
