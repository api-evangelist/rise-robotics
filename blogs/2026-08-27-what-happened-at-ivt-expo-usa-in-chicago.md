We just wrapped two days at iVT Expo USA in Chicago, the largest off-highway vehicle technology show in North America bringing together the engineers, OEMs, and suppliers building the future of our industry. 🦾 Electrification, automation, and AI-enablement of off-highway machinery dominated the conversation at this year's event. Several potential customers stopped by our booth to check out how our Beltdraulic™ cylinders unlock all of these capabilities for heavy machines by delivering hydraulic-class force through an electric, digitally controlled actuation platform.
